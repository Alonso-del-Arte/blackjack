# Strategy suggestions for blackjack

[The rules of blackjack](GameRules.md) allow moves that are inadvisable.

## Splitting

* **Aces** &mdash; There is near universal agreement you should always split 
Aces. A pair of Aces gets counted as 12 so that you don't bust, but if you hit 
on that hand you run the risk of busting if you get a Ten or a royal card. The 
worst case scenario for splitting Aces is that you get more Aces, but at least 
there's no immediate risk of going bust.

* **Eights** &mdash; There is near universal agreement you should always split 
Eights.

* **Nines** &mdash; "I never split Nines," says some guy in a TV ad for an 
online casino. Is he supposed to be a famous blackjack player? Even if he is, I 
wouldn't put much stock in any proscription coming from some guy in an online 
casino ad. Splitting Nines might be a good idea _if_ you think the dealer is 
likely to get to 19, 20 or 21.

* **Tens** or **Jacks** or **Queens** or **Kings** &mdash; Splitting a hand with 
a value of 20 is generally a bad idea. You can stand on 20 and there's a good 
chance the dealer might have to stand on 17, 18 or 19, or go bust, in which case 
you win for having a better score. Splitting makes it likely you might have to 
stand on a low score or go bust.
